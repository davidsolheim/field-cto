---
title: Ore-Max
tagline: Production feedback loop powered by AI agents
order: 1
---
**Problem:** Production user feedback was piling up faster than the team could triage, diagnose, and ship fixes — creating backlog risk and slower response times.

**Approach:** Built an AI-assisted feedback resolution pipeline integrated with Linear and local dev workflows. Agents fetch unresolved production tickets, reproduce issues locally, implement fixes, and prepare merge-ready PRs with verification steps.

**Outcome:** Reduced time from feedback to fix candidate from days to hours. Established a repeatable pattern for AI-native maintenance that leadership could trust in production.
